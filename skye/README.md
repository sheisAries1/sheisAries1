# Skye's World 💛

A cartoon website made for my niece Skye.

- **Hello!** — a day/night sky with drifting clouds. Click Skye and she jumps and says something new.
- **All About Me** — fun fact cards, plus a "sparkle words" button that gives a new compliment each time.
- **Star Catcher** — a 30-second game. Catch stars (+1) and hearts (+5), dodge rain clouds (−2). Your best score is saved.
- **Sky Painter** — a drawing pad with colours, brush sizes and stickers, and a button to save the picture.
- **Sound** — cheerful sound effects everywhere (🔊 turns them off) and a "Twinkle Twinkle" tune (🎵). All sounds are made in the browser, so there are no audio files.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It works on phones, has a night-sky mode, and respects reduced-motion settings.

To change the fun facts, edit the cards in `index.html`. The greetings and sparkle words are in `js/main.js`.
