The Gram stain, one step at a time

Every identification starts here. Step through the four reagents and watch what happens to each type of cell wall.

Gram-positive wall

Gram-negative wall

From the bench: the decolouriser is the step that makes or breaks a film. Too long and Gram-positives go pink; too short and Gram-negatives stay purple. Mycobacteria barely take up the Gram stain at all, so they need a Ziehl-Neelsen (acid-fast) stain.
